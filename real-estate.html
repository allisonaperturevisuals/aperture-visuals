Prep guide

Set the Scene

A little preparation makes a big difference in photos. Use this checklist to get each room shoot-ready before I arrive. The better the prep, the better the final images.

Download PDF checklist →

01

General

Turn on all lights and replace any burnt bulbs.
Open all blinds and window shades to let in natural light.
Turn off all ceiling fans.
Declutter entryways. Remove shoes, bags, and coats from view.
Touch up any scuffs or holes in walls if possible.
Occupants should plan to leave the property for the duration of the shoot.

02

Kitchen

Clear countertops of bulky appliances, papers, and clutter.
Empty the sink and put dishes away.
Remove magnets, notes, and artwork from the refrigerator.
Take out the garbage and move bins out of sight.
Stow dish soap, sponges, and drying racks under the counter.
Remove rugs, trivets, and dish towels for cleaner lines.

03

Living & Dining

Clear the dining table and straighten all chairs.
Remove stacks of magazines, mail, remotes, and clutter.
Fluff and arrange sofa pillows neatly.
Declutter the fireplace mantle to a few intentional pieces only.
Remove children's toys and pet items from view.
Remove personal family photos and any artwork that may not photograph well.

04

Bedrooms

Make all beds neatly. Tuck in sheets and smooth comforters.
Clear nightstands of personal items, keeping only a lamp or plant.
Put away all clothing, shoes, and valuables.
Clear items from under the bed that might be visible in wide shots.
Remove personal photos, posters, and stickers from walls.
Close closet doors unless they open to a walk-in worth showing.

05

Bathrooms

Clear all countertops of toiletries, soap, and personal items.
Empty shower stalls and bathtubs of all products.
Clean mirrors and glass surfaces thoroughly.
Keep the toilet seat and lid closed.
Hang fresh towels neatly and remove floor rugs.
Remove plungers, cleaning supplies, and bath toys.

06

Pets

Remove all food and water bowls from floors.
Stow beds, crates, and toys out of sight.
Remove pet hair from furniture and surfaces.
If possible, arrange for pets to be away during the shoot.
