Allison Kroes

Filmmaker & Cinematographer

Capturing moments
that last forever

I'm Allison Kroes, a filmmaker based in Grand Rapids, Michigan. From age 12, I would record anything and everything, fascinated by the ability to make a moment in time last forever. That early obsession never left me. It became a career.

When it came time to choose a path in college, the answer was already clear. I pursued that passion at Grand Valley State University, where I earned a Bachelor of Science in Film, working across every phase of production from pre-production through post.

An internship at Coastline Studios opened the door to wedding videography and new professional workflows. I was later invited to continue there as a subcontractor after graduating. I also gained deep real estate media experience at Smallegan Real Estate, where I worked alongside agents firsthand and built a strong foundation in property photography and video.

My love for film naturally extended to social media. I've grown an audience of over 40,000 followers and accumulated more than 4 million views by creating content that people genuinely return to.
