Grand Rapids, Michigan

Allison Kroes

Filmmaker & Cinematographer

Capturing moments
that last forever

Hi, I'm Allison! I've been recording pretty much everything since I was 12 — there was just something about a camera being able to freeze a moment and keep it forever. That obsession never really wore off, and somewhere along the way it turned into my whole career.

So when it came time to pick a major, film was kind of a no-brainer. I studied it at Grand Valley State University and got my hands on every part of the process, from the first planning meeting all the way to the final edit.

An internship at Coastline Studios pulled me into wedding videography, and I loved it enough to keep working with them after I graduated. I also spent a ton of time shooting real estate with Smallegan Real Estate — that's where I really learned how to make a property look its absolute best.

That love of film spilled right over into social media, too. I've built an audience of 40,000+ followers and over 4 million views, just by making stuff people actually want to come back to.
